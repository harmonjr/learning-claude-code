# Social Media Content Templates for Real Estate Agents

## Overview
These templates help you create consistent, engaging social media content across platforms. Use Claude Code to fill them in with your specific market data and listings.

```markdown

📈 Homes Sold: [NUMBER]

[2-3 sentences explaining implications for buyers or sellers]

Questions about your home's value in this market? DM me for a free analysis.

#[YourCity]RealEstate #MarketUpdate #RealEstateMarket #[Neighborhood]Homes #LocalMarketExpert
```

**Claude Code Prompt:**
```

```

## Platform-Specific Optimization

### Instagram
- **Image First**: Lead with stunning visuals
- **Hashtags**: 10-15 relevant, mix of popular and niche
- **Stories**: Use daily for behind-the-scenes
- **Reels**: Short-form video for maximum reach
- **Carousel**: Great for listings and educational content

### Facebook
- **Community**: More personal, conversational tone
- **Length**: Can be longer, more detailed
- **Groups**: Share in local community groups (follow rules)
- **Events**: Use for open houses
- **Live**: Go live during open houses

### LinkedIn
- **Professional**: Focus on market insights, business tips
- **Expertise**: Position as market authority
- **Network**: Connect with other professionals, referral sources
- **Articles**: Long-form market analysis

### Twitter/X
- **Brevity**: Quick tips, market stats
- **Frequency**: Multiple times per day
- **Engagement**: Respond quickly to questions
- **Threads**: Multi-tweet deep dives on topics

---

## Best Practices

1. **Consistency**: Post at least 5x per week
2. **Quality over Quantity**: One great post beats five mediocre ones
3. **Engage**: Respond to every comment and DM
4. **Value First**: 80% helpful content, 20% promotional
5. **Use Data**: Reference real market statistics
6. **Be Human**: Show personality and authenticity
7. **Call to Action**: Every post should have a clear next step
8. **Track Performance**: Note which posts get best engagement
